My Master's thesis

High-Performance Computing & Quantum Simulation

Accelerating Quantum Circuit Simulation

Thesis submitted in October 2026, defence pending

I benchmarked six state-vector simulators on three circuit families, from a single CPU thread up to 64 nodes and 16 NVIDIA A100 GPUs on the EuroHPC supercomputer Deucalion, and used performance models and profiling to explain the rankings. No backend wins everywhere, since the leader depends on the circuit, the qubit count and the hardware. The results support a two-level framework for choosing a simulation method and then a backend.

  • 6simulators benchmarked
  • 64CPU nodes on Deucalion
  • 16NVIDIA A100 GPUs
  • Qiskit Aer
  • CUDA-Q
  • Qulacs
  • mpiQulacs
  • Qibo
  • QuEST

Analysed with the cache-aware roofline model (CARM, with PMU counts checked against DynamoRIO) and top-down profiling in Intel VTune.
